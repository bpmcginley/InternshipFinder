## Release and follow-up

Before using a draft, replace the bracketed club greeting, verify the recipient and current roles, and obtain approval for that exact message and destination. Start with one or two relevant organizations, not all eight. Record whether a leader welcomes the resource, asks for changes, declines, or never replies; do not treat silence as permission. Do not repeatedly follow up, mass-message club members, offer rewards for reviews, or add anyone to the digest yourself. Leave any unknown contact on hold.
