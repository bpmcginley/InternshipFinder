      How it works

      • Pick one of your open roles that InternScout already lists, or three, and the day it starts.
      • We email a link to your work address. Open it within 48 hours to confirm the address, then pay through Stripe.
      • We review every paid listing before it goes live, usually within 2 business days. If we can't run it, you get a full refund.
      • It runs for 30 days from your start day. If our review finishes after the start day, the 30 days start from the day we approve it.
      • If the role closes early, you get the unused days back, as a refund or a code for another paid listing, whichever you choose.

      No bidding

      Prices are fixed and there's no automatic renewal. A page shows at most one paid listing, and when several fit, each has the same chance. At most 5 paid listings run at once in a field, so each keeps its share. When a field is full, we show the first day it has room.
