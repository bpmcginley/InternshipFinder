From your posting to a paid listing

  1. Choose an open role. Find your company and select one or three roles. If a posting is missing, ask us to add your board for free.
  2. Choose dates and confirm your email. Review the price and terms, then pay through Stripe after email confirmation. A valid pilot code makes the order free.
  3. We review it before it runs. If we cannot approve your order, you get a full refund. You can also cancel by email for a full refund before approval.

What payment changes

It pays for the separate, labelled box. It does not buy a better ranking.

  • Paying never changes where any listing ranks, how it is scored, which filters it passes or how many results a student sees. Your role stays in its usual place in the results. Auto-Apply works the same for every listing, paid or not.
  • Students can hide paid listings. Their saved profile can hide a role that does not fit, but is never used to select a paid listing. When several paid listings fit, each has the same chance of appearing.
  • We do not promise views, clicks or applicants. After an uninterrupted campaign and its final calendar month end, we email rounded counts of how often the box was shown and opened. You can also ask for whole-month counts. We do not see applications, so we cannot count or attribute them.
  • Your checkout or invoice states the price and dates. If the role closes, changes or leaves InternScout, it stops showing and the unused days are returned as a refund or credit, your choice.
