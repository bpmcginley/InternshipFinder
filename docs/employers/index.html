Your internships on InternScout

InternScout is a free internship search for college students in the US and Canada. It lists 14,000+ open internships, co-ops and research roles in every major, collected several times a day from employers' own job boards. Students search it for free, with no account.

Your internships may already be here

If your company posts internships on a public job board, such as Workday, Greenhouse, iCIMS, Ashby or Lever, InternScout probably lists them already. You don't need to do anything, and it costs nothing.

Each listing links to the posting on your own careers site, so students apply through your system. InternScout takes no applications and doesn't stand between you and an applicant. To check, search for your company on the dashboard or in the list of employers hiring now.

Add a missing board, or fix or remove a listing

If your internships aren't showing up, send us the address of your careers page or job board and we'll look at adding it. If a listing is wrong, has closed, or shouldn't be there, send its link and we'll correct or remove it. That includes the company name, location, pay or dates on a listing.

All of this is free for every employer, whether or not you ever pay for anything. Adding your job board doesn't depend on payment and is never part of what we sell.

Email brucepmcginley@gmail.com, or use the feedback form. InternScout is run by one student, so expect a reply within a few days.

What students use it for

  • Searching every listing and ranking it for their major, class year and the states they pick.
  • Browsing pages for each field, state, major and employer, each linking to the original postings.
  • Filling in applications with the optional, free Auto-Apply Chrome extension. It never submits: the student reviews every answer and presses Submit.

Paid listings: would you want one?

We're planning one paid option: a paid listing. It would show one of your open roles in a separate box headed "Paid listing", with "Ad" before your company name. Here's how it would work, so you know before you ask:

  • It shows only when a student has searched for something the role matches or picked its field, and on our pages for its field or location. There's at most one paid listing on a page.
  • Paying never changes where any listing ranks, how it's scored, which filters it passes or how many results a student sees. Your role also stays in its usual place in the results. Auto-Apply works the same for every listing, paid or not.
  • It isn't aimed at students by their profile, school, major, class year or anything else about them. It follows only what a student has searched for or picked. Students can hide paid listings.
  • We don't promise views, clicks or applicants. You'd get a monthly count of how often the box was shown and opened, rounded, with nothing about any student. We don't see applications, so we can't count them.
  • Your listings, board additions and corrections stay free whether or not you buy a paid listing.
  • Planned price: $29 per role for 30 days, or $69 for three role-months. Nonprofits, government and public university programs: $15, or $35 for three. No bidding and no automatic renewal. If the role closes early, you get the unused days back as credit or a refund.
  • The first five employers get a free 30-day pilot for one role.
