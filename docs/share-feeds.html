Post new internships for your field in a club Discord or Slack.

Choose a feed once. Your channel's feed app can post new roles as it finds them.

1. Find your field's feed

  1. Browse internships and open a field or location page that lists roles.
  2. Near the bottom, find RSS feed. Copy that link's address.
  3. Use the feed link, which ends in /feed.xml, rather than the dashboard's search address.

Feeds link directly to employer postings. They contain recent roles, not a complete archive. Check the posting before applying.

2. Connect your channel

Slack

Add Slack's RSS app to your workspace. Paste your feed URL, choose a channel, then subscribe. If your workspace requires app approval, ask its owner.

With the app installed, you can also send /feed subscribe YOUR_FEED_URL in the channel. Use /feed list to find a feed's ID and /feed remove ID to stop it.

Slack's RSS setup instructions

Discord

Ask a server manager to add an RSS bot such as MonitoRSS. In its web dashboard, add your feed URL and choose a server channel. Manage or remove that connection in the bot's dashboard.

MonitoRSS setup and dashboard

3. Check the first updates

Use a channel your club wants for opportunities. Delivery timing depends on the feed app. If updates stop, open the feed link and check the app's status and channel permissions.

InternScout does not join your channel or post messages itself. You control the connection through the app you choose.
