Terms of use

Last updated September 14, 2026

InternScout is a free tool made by a UMass Amherst student. By using the dashboard or the extension, you agree to these terms.

The short version

  • It's provided as-is, with no guarantees.
  • Listings can be stale or wrong. Check with the employer.
  • Auto-Apply never submits for you. You review and submit.
  • AI use has fair-use monthly caps, and they may change.
  • Don't misuse it.
  • It isn't run by UMass Amherst, and it may change or shut down.

Not affiliated with UMass Amherst

InternScout is an independent student project. It is not made, run or endorsed by the University of Massachusetts Amherst. Sign-in uses your Google or Microsoft account; a verified .edu email only raises your AI allowance and does not mean any school approves this tool.

Listings may be stale or wrong

  • Listings are collected automatically from public job boards and lists.
  • A listing can be closed, out of date, or wrong. That includes its field tags, location, dates, pay and who can apply.
  • Always check the posting on the employer's own site before you apply.
  • We don't screen employers. Never pay to apply, and be careful with any posting that asks for money or bank details.

Auto-Apply and AI

  • Auto-Apply never submits an application for you. It fills in the form and stops at the submit button. You review everything and press Submit yourself.
  • AI can make mistakes. It can get facts wrong or write things that aren't true about you. You are responsible for everything you send to an employer.
  • Employer and application sites have their own terms. You are responsible for following them.
  • Things like CAPTCHAs, email codes and two-step sign-in always need you.

Fair use and monthly caps

  • Search is free and has no caps.
  • AI features are free up to a monthly allowance per student, for example a set number of tailored resumes, Auto-Apply runs and Deep Dives. The extension and dashboard show what you have left.
  • There are also short-term limits on how fast you can make AI calls.
  • If the project's total AI budget for the month runs out, AI features pause for everyone until the next month. Search keeps working.
  • Caps, limits and features may change at any time.
  • An optional paid supporter plan raises the allowance for students who want more. It is optional, and search stays free. See below.

Supporter plan (optional)

This only applies if you choose to subscribe, and only when the plan is switched on. If you don't see an Upgrade button, there is nothing to pay.

  • It exists to cover AI costs, not to make a profit. It raises your monthly AI allowance; it does not change the listings or unlock search.
  • Stripe takes the payment. You subscribe, change your card and cancel on Stripe's own pages. We never see your card details. Stripe's terms apply to the payment itself.
  • It renews monthly until you cancel. Cancel any time on the billing page; you keep the larger allowance until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • No refunds by default, since the cost is already spent on AI calls. If something goes wrong on our side, get in touch and we'll try to sort it out.
  • The price, the allowance it buys, and the plan itself can change or end at any time. If the plan ends, subscriptions are cancelled and the free allowance still applies.
  • The global monthly AI budget and the anti-misuse rules still apply to supporters.
  • To delete your data while subscribed, cancel first — see the privacy policy.

Don't misuse it

You agree not to:

  • Send automated or scripted requests to our server, or use it to scrape anything.
  • Use our server as a general AI service for things unrelated to InternScout.
  • Share, sell or lend your account, sign-in or tokens.
  • Get around the caps, for example with extra accounts.
  • Use InternScout to send spam or mass, low-effort applications.
  • Send false information to employers.
  • Try to break, overload or attack InternScout or any site it connects to.
  • Break the law or another site's terms.

We may limit or block access for anyone who misuses the service.
