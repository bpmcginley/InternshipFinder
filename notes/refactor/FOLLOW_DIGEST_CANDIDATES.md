# Bounded Follow digest expiry candidates

`expireFollowDigestCandidate` is a dormant one-job operation. The current Worker entry, scheduler, delivery owner and public output remain unchanged. It requires literal opt-in and the two indexes added by migration 0033. It returns a status and direct mutation counts without returning a job ID or storing a cursor.

A call reads the database clock once. With that same cutoff, it probes up to three due attempting leases and at most one row each for queued expiry, leased expiry and seven-day age. If a third due attempt exists, the call returns unavailable without a write. With at most two due attempts, the seek has the complete set and selects its minimum signed rowid. The other three seeks supply independently eligible candidates. Only attempting-to-uncertain transitions can advance progress, and all due attempts are ordered by their signed rowids even when lease order differs. An empty candidate scan returns completion separately from the cap hold.

A mutation batch recomputes the indexed selection and checks both due rowids and job identities, plus the selected job's exact rowid, identity, immutable fields and eligibility. Before an uncertain transition, it also checks the exact pending digest, timestamp and raw advance bytes, or their continued absence. The pending check uses the selected job's primary key. If the job requires a state transition and age deletion, the batch first applies the existing transition. The delete then requires the immediately preceding direct write and the same identity and rowid. This preserves finalization before pending data is erased and deletion follows. Both writes require exact `RETURNING` receipts and bounded aggregate changes. Lost or contradictory acknowledgement yields unavailable without a retry or a completion claim. A previously committed write may still have taken effect.
