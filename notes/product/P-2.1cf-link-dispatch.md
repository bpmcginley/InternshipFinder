## Selection and continuation

`runFollowLinkDispatch` requires literal opt-in, explicit positive shared cap, integer `maxCandidates` from 1 through 64, synchronous nondecreasing clock, confirmation presentation, management presentation/link builder and immediate transport. These limits are technical work bounds, not provider quotas. There is no schedule or provider default.

Migration0028 adds only `(expires_at,id) WHERE claim_id IS NULL` partial indexes to the two existing queues. At a fresh SQLite UTC sample, each query seeks beyond expiry or the caller's later keyset and returns at most `maxCandidates` rows. Thus at most twice that many candidates are read and at most `maxCandidates` examined. No residual subscriber/token eligibility predicate precedes LIMIT. Existing delivery functions perform the exact current consent/claim checks. Claimed and expired backlog does not consume the selection window. Each candidate is checked against fresh database and app time before delivery; future-due or newly expired candidates are skipped within the same bound.

The caller supplies and receives separate `{expires_at,id}` continuations and `nextKind`. Both queues share capacity, alternate when nonempty, and use expiry/id order within their own queue. Advance occurs only for an examined candidate, including ineligible/skipped outcomes. Capacity exhaustion preserves the blocked kind's prior cursor and next turn. A queue wraps only after a later query observes no further candidates. This avoids repeatedly blocking at an unclaimed but ineligible head. Continuations are transient scheduling hints, not stored watermarks or consent authority. The future runtime must resume them across ticks. Restarting at the beginning on every invocation defeats this progress guarantee; corrupted rows hold the page rather than silently skipping unverifiable state.

## Attempts and limits

Each examined job reaches its existing delivery function at most once per invocation. Both claim paths debit the same aggregate cap atomically. Handoffs are sequential within this invocation; ambiguous transport stops immediately. No raw credential or rendered body enters the continuation or output counts. Existing committed claims prevent repeated sends after lost acknowledgements. No capacity is refunded or ambiguous operation retried here.

A future scheduler must serialize transport across invocations and resolve an in-flight/uncertain transport boundary before resuming. Candidate bounds do not impose a wall-clock bound on a noncooperative injected transport. Public management URL approval, sender/address/quota, storage/retention evidence, actual provider configuration and rollout remain separate gates. There is no route, scheduler, stored cursor, public copy, new retained field or activation.
