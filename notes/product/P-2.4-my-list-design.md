# P-2.4 Cross-device My List

**Proposed for Bruce's approval.** The browser-only list began its observation period with [#204's Pages run](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/actions/runs/42931119113). Build only after November 24, 2026 at 7:26:35 p.m. Eastern (November 25 at 00:26:35 UTC) and approval of this design and its copy. Release requires coordinated privacy publication and an owner-authorized Worker deploy.

## Experience and proposed copy

| When | Exact proposed words |
| --- | --- |
| Second distinct manual save while signed out | “Keep My list across devices. Sign in to save your list to your account.” Buttons “Sign in” and “Keep on this browser”. Show once per browser, after the save succeeds. |
| Signed-in My list settings | “Save My list to your account?” “Your saved roles and their application statuses will be available on other devices where you sign in.” Use “Open account list” to load an existing synced list. For a new synced list use “Turn on sync” or “Keep on this browser”. Signing in alone leaves sync off on this device. |
| Optional import into an enabled account | “Copy selected saved roles and their statuses from this browser? Existing account entries stay as they are. On a shared computer, check that these roles are yours.” Buttons “Copy selected roles” and “Skip”. |
| Sync off, stale or offline | “Turn off sync and delete my account list”. Confirm with “Delete your account list from all devices? Your separate browser list stays here.” On success “Your account list was deleted.” For one role “Remove role and status from account”. On conflict or lost confirmation “Refresh My list before making another change.” Offline “Reconnect to change your account list.” At the limit “Your account list is full. Remove a role and status before adding another.” |

Keep account and browser lists separate. Hold only the current account list in memory. Clear it on sign-out, session expiry, confirmed list deletion or account switch and ignore late responses. “Delete browser tracker” remains device-only. The [terms](../../docs/terms.html) already advise signing out on shared computers.

## Storage and deletion

- Store one encrypted list per existing Worker account, separately keyed from Deep Dive. Each of up to 1,000 entries contains the public listing ID, saved membership and optional existing application status. Unsave hides a role without clearing its status. Setting status for a new ID saves it, while explicit unsave wins for a known ID. Removing the role and status erases its entry. Snapshots, saved searches and visit timestamps stay on the original device. On a new device, an unavailable role shows “Saved role {id}” and “Listing details unavailable”, with no posting link.
- Import only selected browser entries after confirmation, with account entries winning duplicates. Compare the account generation and server revision and check session validity at commit. After a conflict or lost acknowledgement, refetch without replay. Keep account writes online and discard pending account work on sign-out or switch.
- Turning sync off or using Delete my data deletes the live list. Account deletion also revokes sessions in the same transaction. Reject access after 365 days without a list change and purge within 24 hours. Bind a 10-minute enable grant to the current generation and retain an empty fence for up to 24 hours after disable or expiry. Before reopening after a backup restore, discard restored My List rows and rotate the feature generation. Verify backup retention before release.

**Proposed privacy copy.** Replace the [current browser-only promise](../../docs/privacy.html) for saved roles and statuses and update [Delete your data](../../docs/privacy.html#delete). “Your saved roles and application statuses stay in this browser unless you turn on My List sync. We keep an encrypted account list of listing IDs, saved or unsaved settings and application statuses for roles you change while syncing or explicitly copy from your browser. Unsave keeps the status. You can remove the role and status together. Turning off sync or using Delete my data deletes the live account list. After 365 days without a change, it expires and is deleted within a day. We keep an empty marker for up to a day after sync stops or expires to block old requests.” Add verified backup wording before approval.

**Offline release tests.** Check zero sync requests before opening or enabling the account list, the second-save prompt, explicit import, browser and account separation, two-device membership and status changes, missing roles, stale writes and lost acknowledgements, delayed responses after account switch, account and browser deletion, expiry, and restore without resurrection. Local Worker tests cover authorization, isolation and storage limits.
