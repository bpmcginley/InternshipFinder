# P-2.1bp Atomic linked Follow account deletion

[#521](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/521) builds on the frozen #517 queue ownership and token-consumption interface. It adds prepared Follow deletion statements to the existing `deleteUser` D1 batch. The mounted `DELETE /me` keeps its authentication, subscription preflight, pending top-up response and separate plan cleanup.

The target set is the account's confirmed linked scopes and unconfirmed scopes with an exact owner-intent queue row. A confirmed anonymous scope with pending linkage stays anonymous and keeps its original confirmation and progress. A legacy owner-bound token by itself does not make an unconfirmed scope owned. Anonymous and other-account scopes and jobs, unrelated orphan subscribers, surviving address-wide credentials and the independent digest marker and send cap are preserved.

The batch first removes only affected subscribers with no surviving scope. It then deletes other confirmation credentials for exact scopes about to be removed, while the owner-intent queue rows still identify them. The scope deletion cascades to its queue and progress and uses the existing digest cancellation rules. Remaining account-owned tokens and queue rows are erased last. This order matters because consuming an owned confirmation token can retire its queue row through the 0023 trigger. Scoped deletion capabilities cascade with their confirmation token.

A committed confirmation first makes the scope linked and eligible for deletion. If account deletion commits first, the credential and its job are unavailable. A claimed confirmation loses its final transport fence without a budget refund. The existing linked signup transaction checks live session proof, so a session revoked by deletion cannot create a new owned job. An earlier transport call cannot be recalled. An open top-up guard or a failed Follow cleanup rolls back the entire existing account batch.

Qualification used actual local Workers/D1 and the real account and confirmation routes with denied external fetch. All 36 focused cases passed across the new route suite and the existing top-up, linked request, digest outbox and progress suites. The five new route cases cover both owned queue variants, same-session deletion, claim and confirmation ordering, exact sibling and marker preservation, repeated forget payment rollback, and an injected Follow cleanup failure.

Worker rollout requires verified adoption of migration 0023, including its owner column and token trigger. Linked signup, provider qualification, sending and deployment remain gated. No route contract, policy text, scheduler or binding changed.
