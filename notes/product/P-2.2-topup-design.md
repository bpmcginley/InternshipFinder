## Behavior, records and deletion

The existing `bonus(user_hash, task, granted, used)` balance is non-expiring and spent after the monthly allowance (`referral.js:37-40,114-126`; `limits.js:294-302`). Add a purchase ledger keyed by a unique checkout/payment identity and a run-allocation ledger, so refunds cannot consume unrelated invite credits or another purchase's unused units. Proposed spend order after monthly units: existing invite units first, then oldest purchased units; record and restore the same allocation after a failed run. Reconcile allocation and balance atomically; never restore units revoked by refund or deletion. Bruce must approve this ordering and its retention cost.

Purchase records: account hash, checkout/payment references, approved offer version, amount/currency, grant/use/revoke counts, status, created/settled/resolved timestamps. Allocation records: purchase reference, existing run identity and units; retain while needed to restore a run or reconcile the purchase, then remove with its ledger. Proposed cleanup is 400 days after balance reaches zero and financial resolution, not a claim about legally required retention. While units remain, retain the ledger until deletion. On account deletion, remove balance, allocations and account linkage; retain only irreversible provider-reference fingerprints and terminal state indefinitely for replay protection. Never recreate deleted credits from a late webhook. Resolve pending payment recovery before deletion; communicate any retry requirement using the existing deletion flow. No names, resumes, card data or email addresses in these new tables.

## Settlement and approval gates

Off unless payment capability and a dedicated server-side price secret are configured; expose a boolean capability plus validated display offer. Use payment-mode checkout and preserve the existing Managed Payments choice. Bruce confirms top-up eligibility with Stripe before enabling it. Use a durable top-up checkout attempt and provider idempotency key, separate from the subscription helper that blocks active subscribers. An uncertain creation must reconcile or expire before another attempt; block a second in-flight checkout and reconcile before deletion. Do not expose private price/account identifiers in documents or public configuration.

Verify signatures and authoritative payment status, kind, owner, line item, quantity, currency and amount. The purchase claim and bonus grant must commit atomically. Existing `stripe_events` receipts are removed on handler failure and expire after 30 days (`billing.js:203-240`, `limits.js:577`), so event IDs alone cannot guarantee one grant per purchase. Handle duplicate/concurrent events, delayed payment, refund-before-grant and deletion races. Proposed full successful refund removes all unused units from that purchase; pending or failed refunds do not. Partial refunds and disputes require Bruce's explicit policy before build; keep the feature disabled until decided. Never let a top-up event fall through to subscription or employer settlement.

Validation before release: worker and Workers-runtime tests for atomic grant/retry, delayed/unpaid events, mismatched offers, allocation restoration, refund ordering, duplicates, partial-refund policy, deletion and disabled configuration. A second agent must review the eventual webhook path line by line. Bruce approves this note, merges implementation/policy/terms PRs, creates the Stripe price, and deploys. No real payment is needed for these tests.

Design references: Stripe requires once-per-session fulfillment with payment verification ([fulfillment](https://docs.stripe.com/checkout/fulfillment)); delivery can repeat and arrive out of order ([webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks)); refunds can be partial or pending ([refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds)). These support the proposed safeguards, not approval of this product's price, legal terms or retention period.
