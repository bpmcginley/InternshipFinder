# P-4.4c optional advisor signup

[Draft #373](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/373) implements the optional weekly advisor renderer on exact [#324](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/324). Bruce approved the exact label, explanation, form disclosure and conditional privacy wording in [#328](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/328) on November 11, 2026.

The weekly renderer accepts an explicit optional signup model. Without it, the page stays static and the full 794-file golden build matches. With it, the shared renderer accepts only a Buttondown embed action and offers the unchecked advisor tag beside any independently supplied field. It keeps the email label, submit button, confirmation, unsubscribe and privacy link, native new-tab POST and shared Monday email. The advisor form has no digest analytics marker or persistence. [Synthetic browser evidence](screenshots/P-4.4c/README.md)

The production build does not supply this model. Owner activation requires confirming the existing account's Tags feature and absence of advisor-tag automation, then publishing the approved conditional privacy wording with the enabled form. Account settings, privacy source and delivery remain owner gates.
