# P-4.4c optional advisor signup

The opt-in weekly advisor renderer uses the shared Buttondown form and the copy approved in [#328](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/328) on November 11, 2026. The package is based on exact [#324](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/324) and omits the form by default.

Owner activation requires the existing account's Tags feature and automation check, plus publication of the approved conditional privacy text with the enabled form.
