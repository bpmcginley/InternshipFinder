[PR #283](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/283) stacks on frozen #278 `0cea3e7ac441a12695045e0b028d0cd59eb57a22`. Approved policy remains unchanged. This reader has no production caller, database argument, grant, balance transition or terminal-attempt write. Merge remains needs-bruce.

`readTopupReversalEvidence` consumes fresh internal paid evidence, a private server secret and an explicitly injected transport. It uses billing's existing pinned API helper for GETs only, independently of new-sale enablement. A matching successful captured Charge is read before and after two complete refund/dispute traversals. Every object must match the verified Charge, optional PaymentIntent reference, USD amount bounds and documented type/ID. Disputes must match live/test mode; Refund objects do not expose a documented livemode field. Only id/status/integer amount/currency survives normalization; no provider body, evidence packet, customer data or URL is retained or returned.

Hard per-invocation ceilings: 100 objects per page, four pages per endpoint per pass, two passes, 18 GETs including Charge reads, 256 KiB per streamed body, 1 MiB total response bytes and ten seconds. Callers may shorten, never extend, the deadline. Streams use strict incremental UTF-8 decoding before JSON parsing. Cancellation, timeout, over-budget or malformed data returns only pending, never partial arrays. A transport ignoring abort cannot advance to another request when its result arrives late. Future wiring must account for these ceilings alongside its other requests and enforce authorization/abuse controls; these are local safety ceilings, not measured provider/account quotas.

Queries filter by the verified Charge and use starting_after from the last validated local ID. They never follow returned URLs. Repeated IDs/cursors, empty continuation pages, unsupported pagination, oversized pages, unrelated references, invalid totals/statuses, incomplete traversal and observed changes between passes remain pending. The existing pure decision function validates its exact normalization/status/amount rules using an initial prior state; that provisional disposition is discarded. The future transactional caller must use actual committed loss/refund/hold/deletion state. Larger or cross-currency FX disputes outside the current decision contract remain pending/manual, never clamped.

Stable Charge bookends must agree on refunded amount/full-refund/dispute indicators. Successful-refund totals cannot exceed the Charge aggregate; that aggregate cannot exceed observed succeeded, pending or requires_action refund amounts. Failed/canceled amounts cannot explain the aggregate. This does not assume whether in-flight refunds already contribute to the aggregate. A disputed Charge with no dispute records is unresolved. Complete passes must match canonical membership, statuses and amounts. Only then does the reader return reversal_evidence with both completeness flags.

**Residual race:** Stripe cursor pagination supplies no documented atomic snapshot or revision token. These checks detect observed inconsistencies, not all transitions occurring after or between reads. No event timestamp orders state. Capture durable purchase revision before provider reads, require fresh reads after CAS conflicts, preserve governing hold IDs/terminal facts, and commit settlement/deletion/balances atomically in later slices. In particular, this evidence alone cannot authorize first grant or release. Reconciliation/webhook delivery must handle later provider changes. Oversized or repeatedly changing cases need bounded later retry/manual resolution, not an unbounded loop or stored partial snapshot.

Managed Payments changes who may initiate refunds/disputes, not this evidence contract: Stripe can act independently, and customer refund totals include charged tax. Fees, withheld tax and net proceeds do not determine purchased-unit disposition. No refund request, dispute action, sender/provider call or private account lookup occurred.
