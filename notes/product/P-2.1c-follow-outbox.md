# P-2.1c: dormant digest outbox and daily marker

Stacked on exact green P-2.1b head `697cd52a`; parents stay frozen. **Worker merge remains needs-bruce.** This is digest storage only: no confirmation/management email queue, matching export, router, cron, template, transport, production configuration or deployment is added.

The outbox stores subscriber ID, enqueue UTC day, canonical scope/public role-ID references, timestamps and claim state. No email address, raw bearer, postal address, subject or rendered body is copied into it. All referenced scopes must currently be confirmed. Each subscriber/enqueue-day snapshot is immutable; enqueue returns an ID only for identical active snapshots, and returns null for changed references or terminal snapshots, so a future matcher cannot acknowledge discarded references; a defensive 32-KiB payload bound rejects oversize input without truncating or advancing any watermark. This is a storage bound, not a chosen email-volume quota. The future trusted matcher must validate role membership against its public export.

A five-minute pre-attempt lease serializes known outstanding work. Expired pre-attempt leases can be reclaimed with a new nonce; stale holders cannot act. Beginning an attempt samples an injected clock after email lookup and HMAC preparation, rejects a UTC-day crossing during HMAC preparation, and rechecks the lease, job lifetime, subscriber and every referenced scope against the fresh transaction-construction timestamp. The attempting lease ends at the earliest of five minutes, job expiry or next UTC midnight. In that same D1 transaction, a new keyed HMAC of normalized email and **actual attempt UTC day** must be inserted before the job becomes attempting. Yesterday's delayed job and today's job therefore compete for today's marker. The marker contains only the HMAC and next-midnight expiry, with no subscriber foreign key, address, role or account reference. Unsubscribe/deletion/resubscription cannot reset it.

The injected HMAC key must be a signing key using SHA-256 with at least 256 bits; missing/wrong keys fail closed. No key or production setting is supplied. All dispatchers must use the same stable key while any current-day marker exists; rotation needs an operator plan and must not reset daily deduplication. Tests use clearly synthetic keys only.

After attempting, there is no transition back to queued. Accepted completion erases payload and records acceptance/last-send time. Explicit uncertainty or an expired attempt becomes terminal uncertain and erases payload; an expired holder's late completion cannot change records. A crash after claiming but before sending can miss an email. The claim day is the attempt day, not a provider receipt or delivery date. The future dispatcher must hand off immediately and reauthorize against the same UTC day and current lease; it must never send from a stale/expired lease. Storage timestamps describe transaction construction, not an unbounded later DB/provider completion. Already accepted mail cannot be recalled.

An SQL scope-deletion trigger cancels **every outstanding aggregate** for that subscriber, including leased/attempting rows, and erases its payload. It preserves accepted records and the independent marker. Full subscriber deletion cascades outbox records. Cancelled/expired/deleted rows no longer hold the known-work lease; the future transport must serialize provider calls and manage any already in-flight call independently of row cancellation. Cancellation does not rebuild that day's snapshot. The future matcher must handle cancellation/watermarks deliberately, and the future dispatcher must reauthorize immediately before transport; this PR does not implement a transport fence or claim that an already in-flight send can be recalled.

Job authorization expires at 48 hours and markers expire logically at next UTC midnight. Cleanup cancels expired jobs, removes subscriber-linked outbox rows seven days after creation, and physically deletes expired markers when invoked. Physical deletion is not automatic at midnight: integration must schedule prompt cleanup even with sending disabled and make published retention promises match measured cleanup behavior. No cron is wired here. The schema addition is migration `0003_follow_digest_outbox.sql` plus the equivalent legacy-schema append; prior migrations are unchanged. Provider limits, shared send-attempt cap/order-mail reserve, postal/sender onboarding, disclosure publication and operator deployment remain prerequisites for any sending integration.

Local tests cover concurrent claims, actual-day rollover, normalized-address deletion/resubscription, consent deletion, lease expiry, rollback, retention and a deletion interleaving between email lookup and atomic claim. No real email, account data, remote DB or provider call is used.
