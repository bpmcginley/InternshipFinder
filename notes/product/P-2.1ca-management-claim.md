# P-2.1ca: dormant management claim and credentials

Draft [#536](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/536) is stacked on frozen #535 `b752c589`. Owner: audit_worker_extension. The #535 author released the overlapping source, schema/parity and registry paths for this separate child. This package has no sender, renderer, provider request, public route, configuration, visible words or activation.

`claimFollowManagementRequest(db, jobId, {enabled, cap})` requires literal opt-in and an explicit positive safe-integer shared send cap. It reads one existing live unclaimed job and subscriber with a stored follow. Fresh management and unsubscribe credentials and the claim UUID are private to that call. It reuses `createFollowCapability` and the existing shared-budget SQL builders. It does not call the separate legacy mint helper, which cannot couple replacement to a budgeted job claim.

One D1 batch observes current database UTC, claims the exact job/subscriber/email/queued/expiry tuple under the shared cap, immediately debits on the successful claim, deletes only that subscriber's old management tokens, and creates a management credential expiring exactly 30 minutes from SQLite issuance plus a new unsubscribe credential. Prior unsubscribe links stay valid. A final database receipt rechecks the current job, subscriber, scopes, exact token hashes/generations, expiry and same UTC day. The old management token remains unchanged if the transaction fails or the claim is ineligible. Atomic completion is not a promise to send: a later expired final receipt returns unavailable even if the claim and budget committed.

Migration0027 adds only nullable `claim_id`, within the approved outbox claim/attempt state. The legacy schema has identical SQLite ALTER output. The expired-request UPSERT resets this field only when replacing an expired row with a genuinely new request. A live duplicate keeps its job, claim and original expiry. Subscriber deletion cascades through jobs and tokens. No email address, bearer, rendered body, account link or provider identifier is added to storage.

Every receipt must be dense, successful and correctly typed. A missing/malformed acknowledgement, including a genuinely committed transaction with a lost receipt, returns no credentials and never retries, refunds the cap or reconstructs raw credentials through readback. A repeat call uses fresh internal IDs and cannot reclaim a spent job. Delete-token counts may exceed one for pre-existing management records, while claim, debit and new credential writes must each report exactly one. Returned address and raw credentials exist only in the transient successful result.

Future delivery must validate renderer/transport settings before claiming, bind its links to these exact credentials, freshly recheck the current job/claim/token/subscriber/expiry/day immediately before transport, invoke once and never retry ambiguous acceptance. This issuance result is not a reusable transport permit. Request lifetime/cooldown, production caps, scheduled physical cleanup, provider retention/quota, postal address, management page and rollout remain separately gated. No policy or private operator values are chosen here.
