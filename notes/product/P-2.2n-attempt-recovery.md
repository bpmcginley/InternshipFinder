[PR #311](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/311) on frozen #303 `c299183a`. Needs Bruce for merge; no activation. Owns a new `worker/src/topup-attempt-recovery.js`, focused shared Node/local D1 tests and registration, this note and its tracker rows. No schema, route, billing, authentication, existing-purchase reconciliation or run lifecycle changes.

`recoverTopupAttempt` composes the existing active-attempt revision capture, historical-offer payment reader, complete reversal reader, first-settlement and authoritative expiry helpers. The historical offer and private key are snapshotted before awaits; the durable revision is captured before either authoritative read. Transport is explicitly injected and permits only GET without a body. Sales enablement and current price availability do not gate reconciliation of historical payments. Missing/mismatched configuration or ambiguous provider evidence cannot grant.

The same verified payment object supplies Charge, PaymentIntent, Checkout, mode, currency and captured-total identity to the reversal reader and settlement. Complete reversal evidence selects the already-approved available/held/revoked initial disposition through the existing atomic helper. A fresh valid clock is sampled after both readers, only for first settlement. Authenticated expiry uses the existing guarded expiry helper. Storage errors propagate; rollback is not reported as success.

Unknown creation without an attached Checkout ID or trusted candidate remains pending. A candidate is only a trusted recovery hint; the payment reader must authenticate its full association. No browser redirect or asserted status is evidence. Results are internal: `inactive` means no active revision could be captured, not proof of payment or permission to delete; `pending` retains uncertainty; `stale` requires a later fresh capture and fresh reads. There is no automatic retry, discovery, reopening, scheduler or existing-purchase reconciliation. A deleted/resolved attempt cannot be recreated by this coordinator.
