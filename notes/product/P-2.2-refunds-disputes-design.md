## Exact proposed replacement refund/deletion terms

> A partial refund does not change your extra runs. Once successful refunds cover the full payment, we remove only the unused runs from that purchase. Pending, failed or canceled refunds do not change your runs. While a payment dispute is active, you cannot use the unused runs from that purchase. If the dispute ends in our favor, we make those held runs available again unless the payment was fully refunded or your account was deleted. If the dispute is lost, we remove those unused runs. If that decision later changes, contact us to review the removed runs. Runs already used, your monthly allowance, invite credits and other purchases are unchanged. Deleting your account removes any extra runs left in it and does not itself request a refund. Contact us before deleting if you need help with a payment.

No new refund entitlement, timing promise, automatic refund request or email is proposed. Other approved offer/privacy wording stays unchanged.

## Proposed state and ordering

- Match the verified top-up purchase, charge, currency, amount and owner, not merely a customer. Sum distinct refund IDs whose current status is `succeeded`, in integer currency units. A partial successful total below the captured charge total leaves units unchanged; a total covering that charge, including charged tax, revokes only this purchase's unused units, including held units. Do not subtract processor fees or count a refund twice. `pending`, `requires_action`, `failed` and `canceled` contribute zero; a request or `charge.refunded` notification alone is not proof of full successful refund. Inconsistent totals require reconciliation.
- Formal active dispute statuses `needs_response`, `under_review`, `appeal_needs_response` and `appeal_under_review` hold all unused units from this purchase, even for a partial monetary dispute. Held units cannot be spent. Inquiry-only `warning_needs_response`/`warning_under_review` do not hold units. Verified `won`, `warning_closed` or `prevented` releases only held units when no other active dispute, prior loss, full refund or deletion blocks them. Verified `lost` revokes unused units; it never creates a negative balance. A later appeal does not automatically recreate units already revoked on loss; operator reconciliation is required. These distinctions are part of the requested approval.
- Keep per-purchase available, held, consumed and revoked units with a fixed grant ceiling. In one transaction, record each state transition and update its balance/allocation. Deletion or confirmed full refund takes precedence over release; loss takes precedence over an active hold. A failed run returns its exact allocation to available or held according to the current purchase state, never to a refunded, lost or deleted purchase. Never borrow or revoke another purchase, invite credit or monthly unit. Grant arriving after refund/loss/deletion yields no spendable units; grant during an active dispute yields held units.
- Signature-verified events trigger reconciliation of current provider objects and the complete refund list. Serialize reconciliation per purchase, with a durable revision/conditional commit so stale concurrent reads cannot overwrite a newer result; retry after conflict. Event IDs suppress duplicate deliveries but do not replace durable purchase/refund/dispute identities. Do not order by arrival or event timestamp. Existing revocations/deletion markers survive late events. Unknown statuses or incomplete reads cannot release held units or authorize a new grant; retain the last verified allocation and retry. Confirmed transitions commit atomically with their durable receipt. Retain only the already-approved fields/fingerprints and retention schedule, adding held units to the same purchase accounting; unresolved disputes prevent cleanup.
