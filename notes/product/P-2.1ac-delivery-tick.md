# P-2.1ac bounded dormant delivery tick

Draft [#361](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/361) on exact #357 `8d356a533a815827e57c2d169dae557cbffca936`. `runFollowDeliveryTick` takes the database, explicit positive candidate limit, clock and existing delivery dependencies/cap. It returns only status plus aggregate outcome counts. It chooses no schedule, cap default, durable cursor, cleanup, provider, route, storage schema or activation policy.

One advisory compound SELECT explicitly uses `follow_digest_outbox_due (state, lease_until)` for queued candidates followed by expired-lease candidates, with one overall LIMIT. There is no global sort, expiry-filtered scan, extra lookahead or FIFO/fairness guarantee. The whole bounded result is validated before use. An expired candidate stops the tick without deletion or scanning past it. This intentionally leaves cleanup and an obstructing expired prefix to a separate retention operation; no job is silently discarded and no persistent cursor skips it.

Worker time must be valid, monotonic and on the tick's initial UTC day. A successful authoritative SQLite UTC read must be on that day, no earlier than the preceding Worker observation or prior database observation. Checks occur before/after the page and after each delivery. Every candidate also needs an unexpired job deadline under both observations. The advisory page cannot authorize sending: each job goes sequentially through unchanged #357 lease, pinned preparation, budget and final consent/recipient/SQLite deadline fences. Competing ticks rely on those existing atomic claims; no new lock or retry mechanism is added.

Paused or unavailable results stop the page immediately. Accepted or terminal uncertain outcomes can be followed by the next candidate within the explicit bound. Previously completed outcome counts survive a later failed clock/database admission. The result is not a delivery receipt or continuation cursor, and processed means this bounded page was examined, not that all due jobs are exhausted. The caller must not use it to permanently advance #354's subscriber enumeration or any matching watermark. Existing #357 presentation, token-link authorization, immediate-or-busy transport and no-retry constraints remain unchanged. Future scheduling must separately coordinate retention, archive batches and sender/operator gates.
