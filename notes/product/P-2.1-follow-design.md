## Storage, matching and deletion

- Subscriber: normalized email, random ID, creation/confirmation/last-send timestamps. Subscription: subscriber ID, kind (`company` or `search`), public employer key or canonical field/state filter keys, optional `owner_user_hash`, created/confirmed timestamps. Reject arbitrary personal text; deduplicate identical follows and aggregate all matches per address.
- P-2.1a implementation clarification, preserving the approved scoped-consent wording: "Link these follows" links only the scopes explicitly confirmed in that request, never prior unlinked scopes for the same email. Account deletion removes those linked scopes and their scope tokens, invalidates pending linkage tokens for that account, and retains the subscriber/general unsubscribe link only while other scopes remain. The account hash is therefore stored per subscription, replacing the original subscriber-level proposal.
- Purpose-scoped tokens: hash, generation, expiry. Confirmation lasts 7 days; management 30 minutes; unsubscribe until deletion. Mint links at dispatch; never persist raw bearer tokens or rendered bodies. No addresses/tokens in logs, counters or exports.
- Durable outbox: subscriber ID, kind, public role IDs/date snapshot, unique subscriber/day key, creation/expiry, claim/attempt state and accepted timestamp. Render plain text at dispatch through `mail.js`. Recheck active consent immediately before dispatch. Delete pending confirmations at 7 days and stale digest jobs at 48 hours; erase payload after acceptance and delete subscriber-linked outbox records after 7 days. Retain last-send only while subscribed. Serialize sends; do not retry an ambiguous provider acceptance, avoiding duplicate daily sends. Document the resulting possible missed email.
- Daily cron matches confirmed follows against one small public first-seen role export. Start the matching watermark at confirmation; invalid exports never advance it. Atomically claim at most one digest per normalized address per UTC day using a separate keyed-HMAC email/day marker. Keep it across unsubscribe, account deletion and resubscription until the next UTC midnight, then delete it; it contains no address, role or account ID. This proposed retention exception needs privacy approval. Reuse outbox leases, not seven-year financial retention.
- Unsubscribe uses a deletion-only bearer link and removes every subscription, token and queued job for the address in one transaction, preserving only that day's digest marker; management permits selective removal or full deletion. Deleting the final follow deletes its subscriber. Link-prefetch safety and standards-compatible one-click unsubscribe must be verified before implementation is accepted. In-flight accepted mail cannot be recalled.

## Decisions and release gate

**Saved-search scope needs a decision.** Field/state matching excludes dashboard text, class year, remote, pay and other filters. This narrows saved-search follows; Bruce must approve this restriction or require a fuller design before implementation. The disclosure above is not approval.

**Account deletion needs a decision.** Current authentication exposes a provider/sub hash, not an email (`auth.js:128-150`); `DELETE /me` uses that identity (`index.js:227-234`). Proposed explicit, confirmed, nullable account linkage means account deletion removes linked follows only. This narrows the hand-off's universal deletion requirement and needs Bruce's approval; otherwise design verified-email linking before building. Never attach an existing unlinked subscription without inbox confirmation.

Sending stays off by default until Bruce approves the copy, privacy paragraph, retention (including the daily marker), saved-search scope, linkage decision and postal address, then verifies the deployed binding, sending-domain onboarding, recipient entitlement and actual account quota. [Cloudflare quotas](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/limits/) are account-specific; [sender restrictions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/configuration/send-bindings/) apply. Set a shared send-attempt cap covering confirmations, management and digests well below the verified quota, reserving capacity for existing order mail. No volume is proposed before that check; all request routes also need bounded abuse controls. Missing configuration disables signup and sending. Bruce owns merge/deploy; no implementation precedes note approval.
