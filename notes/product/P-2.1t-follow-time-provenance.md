# P-2.1t Follow first-seen provenance

The fixed-export capture can accept a trusted in-process attestation for naive `first_seen` values. An attestation binds to the exact index and all shard digests. Each entry binds one deduplicated open row ID and the exact raw timestamp string to one of two claims.

- **Verified UTC origin.** An aware instant normalizes to the exact naive wall value. The ordinary export-generation and millisecond window checks still apply.
- **Prior observation.** An aware upper bound proves this same observation already existed at or before the aligned lower boundary. The role is excluded without assigning a timezone to its historical timestamp.

Each naive open row needs one unambiguous claim in provenance mode. Evidence for a closed, aware, or absent row is rejected. The strict default still requires aware timestamps. The existing capture-wide legacy diagnostic option remains explicit and cannot be combined with the per-row attestation.

The caller must independently establish writer origin or the time of a prior observation and verify continuity through the writer's actual carry path. A commit hash, matching ID alone, or matching timestamp text alone does not prove that time or continuity. No writer currently constructs this evidence. Missing or contradictory evidence rejects the capture before it can produce snapshot bytes or advance a watermark. The output format and Worker matcher are unchanged.

Future wiring needs a verified capture source and aligned starting boundary. This package does not infer historical timezones, backfill coverage, publish an archive, or enable email.
