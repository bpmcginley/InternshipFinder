# P-2.2ai Top-up sales readiness

[#458](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/458) is based on [#442](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/442) as merged to main in `42ad3f21`. Initial qualification used `e2a2621d`, followed by the normally integrated `b68af16d` successor. Both merges preserve the exact Worker source, test registries, and the two package tracker rows.

New sales require the existing top-up settings, `FUNDED_RUNS_MODE=create`, `FUNDED_PURCHASES=1`, and the response limits accepted by funded AI admission. The limit settings are `FUNDED_RESPONSE_MAX_BYTES`, `FUNDED_RESPONSE_EVENT_BYTES`, and `FUNDED_RESPONSE_DEADLINE_MS`. The same checks gate the optional dashboard configuration hint, offer verification and Checkout creation. Offer and Checkout routes recheck sales readiness after account and budget reads, immediately before and after the bounded Stripe Price read, and after the second account check.

An unavailable sales configuration omits the hint and keeps the existing disabled response for authenticated sales requests. Sales routes make no provider call when the gate is closed before verification. A gate that closes during verification discards the verified Price before reservation or further provider calls. The account, session, Origin, Price, budget, allowance and response rules already in the routes still apply.

Signed historical top-up settlement, reversal and expiry continue under the existing recovery settings with new sales off. The default legacy AI route still uses its legacy admission path. This package does not enable sales, change payment terms, or add a production setting, schema change, public data field, or UI.

The three added shared regressions fail against the frozen parent. They cover thirteen unusable configurations, a readiness change during Price for each sales route, and readiness changes during either account read. The existing positive routes verify display-only terms, Checkout and replay, and retain the session and budget tests. All input and provider responses are synthetic. The original full Node suite passed 755 tests. On the integrated Worker tree, 40 focused Node and 41 serial local Workers/D1 tests passed, including the smoke and Checkout deadline cases. An optional broad parallel Workerd run was interrupted after timing failures. Independent review, exact CI and coordinated owner release remain pending.
