# P-2.2g dormant authoritative payment evidence

[PR #278](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/278), stacked on frozen #272 `4c11ac094a551717ed6a79b39a02e144cb35dbd1`. Approved top-up policy remains $3 USD before applicable tax for 25 units. This build remains needs-bruce; it neither grants units nor changes an attempt's state.

`readTopupPaymentEvidence(env, attempt, originalOffer, fetch, candidateCheckoutId)` uses at most three GETs through billing's existing pinned `2026-08-26.dahlia` helper. Fetch must be injected; no default provider transport exists. The original offer is a trusted private version-to-price/mode mapping with the approved amount/currency/units. It must come from server configuration, never request metadata. No mapping or private identifier is created or stored here. Recovery intentionally ignores new-sale switches, active-price status and current subscription offers; an archived original price can still identify a payment. Unknown historical mappings stay unresolved instead of guessing from today's configuration.

The reader snapshots input, verifies Checkout identity/mode/live-test/account/attempt/offer metadata, the exact original price and quantity, USD subtotal, exclusive-tax behavior and consistent tax total. Paid evidence additionally requires complete/paid Checkout, a freshly read succeeded PaymentIntent with matching association and full received amount, and its freshly read successful captured Charge with matching identity, currency and full captured amount. The output separates `offerAmount` from tax-inclusive `capturedAmount`. It excludes provider bodies, secrets, payment details and URLs. Redirects are blocked and failures return only `pending` before billing can log private provider paths/errors.

Results are `pending`, `paid_evidence` or `expired_evidence`. Expiry requires an authoritative expired/unpaid session and either no PaymentIntent, or a matching canceled intent with zero received/capturable amount and no charge or a matching failed, unpaid, uncaptured charge. Open/processing/ambiguous/inconsistent states remain pending regardless of age. An independently known candidate ID can recover a creating row whose response was lost, but only after full provider association; it cannot replace an existing different checkout ID. An expired stored attempt cannot produce paid evidence, and a settled one cannot produce expiry evidence. None of these results reopens or writes a row.

**Required before settlement:** paid evidence is insufficient to grant. A refunded or disputed charge can still prove successful capture. Complete current refund/dispute paging, the approved decision function, and an atomic first-settlement transaction must assign available/held/revoked units and mark the attempt together. Existing-purchase revision capture must precede provider reads; stale CAS requires new reads. Account deletion and unresolved-attempt barriers must serialize with settlement and admission. No event timestamp or browser redirect establishes payment or orders revisions. Signed webhook routing, bounded reconciliation scheduling, historical configuration recovery and physical retention/deletion remain later slices. This reader makes no complete-refund/dispute claim and has no database argument or production caller.

Baseline: 409 Node tests pass. Final: all 415 Node tests, six focused synthetic scenarios, pinned JavaScript lint, new-file formatting and diff check pass. Tests verify GET-only paths/version, disabled-sale and archived-price recovery, tax separation, candidate association, delayed payment, expiry contradictions, cross-purchase/mode/amount failures, malformed reads and safe output. This adds no SQL; local D1 changes/testing are not applicable to this evidence-only slice. Parent financial modules, provider routes, schema/migrations, public assets and goldens remain byte-identical. No new visible strings, UI screenshots or startup-budget change. No actual provider requests, payments, remote DB, deployment, release or merge.
