# P-2.1o budgeted digest attempt

[PR #310](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/310) is stacked on exact #302 `4456afbc`. Needs-bruce. The strict snapshot claim is dormant and has no transport caller.

A future caller prepares the pinned snapshot, builds and validates its envelope, then calls `beginBudgetedFollowSnapshotAttempt` at the attempt boundary. It supplies the existing HMAC key, exact job and lease, fresh clock, and explicit positive safe-integer shared cap. No default is installed. Missing cap, wrong lease, missing immutable snapshot metadata or stale scope generations cannot authorize a claim. The existing unbudgeted APIs retain their original behavior for dormant callers.

The helper samples UTC after computing the daily HMAC. One D1 batch observes the time through #302's monotonic clock fence, checks room under the cap and exact live lease, recipient and current snapshot consent, inserts the existing daily marker, marks the job attempting, then debits the shared aggregate. A duplicate marker or failed claim cannot debit the cap. A failing write rolls back all writes. No new table, stored identity or retention is introduced.

A capped job returns `paused` only under a still-live pre-attempt lease. The scheduler should stop the current pass without immediate retries. That lease remains reclaimable after its normal expiry. No marker, debit, cancellation, payload erasure or watermark advance occurs because of the cap. If cap room exists, the legacy cancellation behavior for a rejected leased attempt still applies. The next UTC day can admit a still-valid job under normal lease and consent rules.

`claimed` means the batch confirmed a durable marker, attempt and debit, and a fresh clock sampled after D1 is still monotonic and before UTC midnight and the original and resulting lease and job deadlines. The batch reads the original deadlines before the attempt can extend the lease. It does not guarantee future consent after that boundary. If the reply is lost, the clock rolls back, or an attempt expires while the batch response is pending, the helper returns `unavailable` and authorizes no send. A committed attempt stays charged and follows the existing uncertainty and no-retry rules. No compensation or reissue is attempted.

Synthetic SQLite and actual local D1 cover last-slot competition with the shared reservation helper, same-day duplicate and resubscription, lower caps, exact UTC rollover, missing pending metadata, consent changes and deletion between read and claim, stale timestamps and deadlines, rollback injected into the debit, and response loss after commit. The independent-review regression reproduces a D1 response crossing the original lease deadline while the extended attempt lease remains live. Node 22.23.4 passed all 432 Worker tests and actual local Workers/D1 passed all 67. Existing legacy claim, outbox and budget tests pass. No production entry, schedule or mail transport imports this helper.

Future integration still needs bounded immutable snapshot retrieval, recipient/link/envelope binding, separate confirmation and management admission, cleanup and dispatch orchestration, approved sender/postal and quota configuration with the order-mail reserve, consent-safe routing, operator deployment and owner approval. No network, provider, export, route or schema changes are part of this package.
