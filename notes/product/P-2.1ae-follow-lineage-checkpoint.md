# P-2.1ae Accepted follow lineage checkpoint

Bruce approved the exact [P-2.1ab decision](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/358) at `b34ea29b03aaadfd31a3a28e0b364c31386ce578` in chat. This slice implements the accepted-record codec and the predecessor reconstruction. Pending generations, durable compare-and-set, storage, writer integration and activation remain separate.

The encoder requires the typed #348 accepted receipt and the original full #344 writer proof from the same trusted process. It compares generation, coverage, source digests and exact naive-open evidence, then verifies every supplied lineage tuple against the actual accepted source bytes. The #348 receipt alone cannot authenticate the closed map. The future pre-push handoff must preserve the original full proof. This codec does not accept untrusted or deserialized receipt substitutes.

The canonical versioned record binds the accepted commit and public subtree, exact index and slim-shard digests, trusted accepted time, aligned coverage start and sorted evidence tuples. UTC instants use six fractional digits. Each row carries the original raw stamp and either a verified UTC origin or an independently bounded prior observation. The raw URL is never serialized. The decoder verifies it from the independently trusted accepted shard bytes and rebuilds both listing-ID/raw-URL associations and the exact prior-lineage keys for #344. Same-stamp aliases may coexist. Contradictory shared-URL facts, unknown naive open evidence and any missing or invalid source association fail closed. Evidenced closed rows survive and can prove a later reopening.

Callers supply explicit checkpoint and source byte/row limits and trusted observation times. The accepted record expires at seven days, exclusive, from the accepted time. The codec does not refresh it. The existing fixed-export rules still bound and validate the raw shards. Wrong identity, malformed or noncanonical records and expired or future time fail without returning lineage. No storage, filesystem, Git, workflow, public output or provider operation is performed by this module. Refactor writer release and owner configuration remain gated.
