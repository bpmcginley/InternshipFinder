# P-2.2 manual review of top-up history gaps

This source slice follows the approved manual-review path in PR #679. It adds a dormant
server-only database reader for purchase records that need review. Public purchases and
existing account deletion are unaffected. There is no HTTP route, CLI, scheduler, new
record, provider call, write, or deletion authority.

The reader scans a fixed-size indexed page of purchase fingerprints in definite key order.
It reports only the internal purchase fingerprint, current finance revision, and two bounded
statuses describing available cleanup timing and evidence. Keys and cursors belong inside the
authorized operator workflow. Do not publish or persist them in the repo, logs, analytics,
support tickets, or a public response. A page can have no candidates and still have a next page.
The age status is pending, not due, or mature at the database's 400-day cutoff. The evidence
status separates missing payment evidence, missing or insufficient recorded coverage, a
younger purchase still under observation, and a purchase whose stored coverage warrants
further verification. The reported record is current only as of the read. A mature record or
matching epoch has no authority to approve erasure. Existing migrations through 0034 are needed
to list coverage cleanly. An unavailable read is not an empty page.

An operator first rechecks one record using the existing authoritative purchase recovery
functions and a new finance revision. Verify refund/dispute state with the approved provider
readers. Verify any claimed history against independent provider/restore evidence. A quiet
event stream, a similar current snapshot, or an operator acknowledgment cannot substitute.
Only existing atomic retirement may erase a record on fresh matching evidence. If that proof
cannot be established, leave the record pending. Use the existing account-deletion flow when
a customer asks to delete their account.
