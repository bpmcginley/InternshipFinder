# P-2.1ad Dormant follow retention maintenance

`maintainFollowRetention(db)` is a bulk internal operation for a future operator integration. It uses one canonical UTC sample from the bound database. The operation applies the existing follow expiry and outbox expiry statements and the existing send-budget observation in one D1 batch. All statements use the same sampled time. Existing standalone callers retain their behavior and defaults.

Existing policy removes expired tokens, pending scopes and orphan subscribers. It makes expired durable attempts uncertain, cancels expired queued or leased jobs, and removes old jobs and expired day markers. Existing triggers advance exact surviving progress for uncertain durable attempts and erase terminal pending data. Queued work that never attempted delivery does not advance progress. The budget resets only when observing a later UTC day. A newer concurrent observation fences out an older sample without resetting the budget.

The result reports successful maintenance and whether the budget accepted the sample. An invalid clock response, storage error or unverified batch response is unavailable. D1 rolls back a rejected batch. An ambiguous response does not prove the transaction's outcome, and the helper does not retry or compensate.

Local tests use actual SQLite and Workers/D1 and cover deletion, uncertain and cancelled progress, UTC rollover, a newer budget fence, full rollback after a late failure, and malformed clock or batch responses. There is no new retention policy, caller clock, route, scheduler, sender, migration or index. This bulk operation is separate from the bounded delivery tick in #361.
