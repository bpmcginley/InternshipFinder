# P-2.1f dormant follow matching: draft producer contract

[PR #255](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/255) stacks on frozen #243 `7ffca463340a81f2dc3987a2c05d350e115a5884`, needs-bruce. This is a pure validator/matcher and synthetic tests, not a published feed, chosen production lookback/size policy or sending implementation. The approved scope remains company or field/state only; text/year/remote/pay filters are excluded. No existing module, shared registry, schema, generator, route, network, dispatcher or public policy is changed.

## Proposed version-one public envelope

UTF-8 JSON bytes: `{version:1, complete:true, generated_at, covers_after, roles:[...]}` with exactly those keys. `generated_at` and `covers_after` are canonical `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ` instants; the complete eligible interval is **(covers_after, generated_at]**. The caller independently supplies the exact authoritative `upperBound`, which must equal `generated_at`. No timestamp comes from a wall clock or a missing/naive date fallback. The module bounds bytes before fatal UTF-8 decoding. The eventual bounded reader must also stop oversized network bodies before buffering them; no fetch/URL is selected here.

Every role has exactly `id`, `company_key`, `field_tags`, `states`, `first_seen`, `status`, `company_name`, `title`, `location`, `apply_url`. IDs retain `normalize.listing_id`'s existing 16 lowercase SHA-256 hex format (`normalize.py:271-292`), also required by `follow-outbox.js:payloadOf`; they are never database row numbers or newly derived identities. The producer must reuse the backend's canonical employer key and field/state semantics, retain authoritative first-seen instants, and emit only eligible organic open roles in the declared interval. Do not stamp missing first-seen dates with build time, truncate to a top-N list, use the purchasable-only role index, or include personal/account data. Duplicate IDs, including identical repeats, invalidate the entire snapshot.

Display facts align with independent renderer #256: `company_name`, `title`, `location`, `apply_url`. Facts must be nonempty single-line trimmed text, with renderer ceilings of 200/300/300 JavaScript units and an explicit UTF-8 budget. Posting links use the renderer's HTTP(S), no-userinfo/unsafe-character, raw/canonical 2,000-character boundary. This validator cannot independently establish that a supplied complete flag or eligibility claim is truthful: the future trusted producer/reader must verify complete coverage, listing eligibility and source authenticity. No production exporter exists for this contract yet.

Caller inputs are trusted canonical `companies`, `fields`, `states` sets and positive safe-integer limits: `maxBytes`, `maxRoles`, `maxFollows`, `maxMatches`, `maxResultBytes`, `maxTextBytes`, `maxWindowMs`. There are no numeric production defaults. The catalog must cover valid existing saved scopes even when their companies have no roles in this recent window; never derive it only from these rows. Production values, publication path, freshness/recovery policy and any schema integration remain separately reviewed export work under refactor ownership.

## Matching and safe progress

Follow inputs contain exactly `{scope_key, confirmed_at, watermark}`. Reuse `canonicalFollow` for the already-approved canonical scopes, including no REMOTE search key. Unconfirmed entries with null watermark are ignored. Company matching is exact key equality. Fields OR together, states OR together, and the two dimensions AND together; an empty dimension is unrestricted. For each confirmed scope, use **max(confirmed_at, watermark)** as the exclusive lower bound, or confirmation when no watermark exists. A role at the exact upper bound is eligible; one at the lower bound is not. Parsed first-seen instants are validated once and reused.

Successful results contain unique matched role objects in producer order, per-scope `{scopeKey, roles:[id...]}` references, and proposed advances `{scopeKey, confirmedAt, from, to}` where `to` is exactly the snapshot upper bound. Deduplicate roles across scopes; renderer #256 additionally deduplicates posting URLs. A valid zero-match result proposes progress but no digest. This module writes nothing: the future caller must recheck consent, compare the captured confirmation/old watermark, and atomically coordinate durable queue claims with progress; queue rejection (including its existing 32 KiB payload bound), cancellation or an unresolved send never implicitly advances here.

Any invalid, incomplete, oversized, conflicting or stale/out-of-coverage input returns only `{status:'invalid', reason}` with no roles/references/advances. A scope whose effective lower bound predates coverage stays fail-closed until an authoritative wider recovery snapshot or explicitly approved operator recovery is available; never silently clip to the recent window and skip roles. Watermark persistence, recovery, pinned snapshot/dispatch handoff, producer completeness proof, daily send limits, disclosure/postal/quota gates and owner deployment remain unimplemented.
