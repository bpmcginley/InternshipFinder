# P-2.1b: dormant follow management

Stacked on the exact reviewed P-2.1a head `63c3e0fa`. This implements only the already approved management-link behavior; **worker merge still needs Bruce**. No schema, router, cron, transport, provider, outbox, UI, published policy or production configuration changes.

Management tokens use the existing purpose/hash/generation storage, expire exactly 30 minutes after minting, and cannot confirm or link subscriptions. Reissue invalidates the previous management generation. Unsubscribe links remain independent and valid until deletion. Management can inspect pending and confirmed scopes without activating them. Listing returns only canonical scope, creation and confirmation timestamps, never the subscriber email, account hash, bearer or stored token hash.

Reading/listing is non-mutating. A later GET/prefetch handler must call only those paths. Explicit management actions can remove one scope or delete all scopes for the inbox. The token remains usable for further actions within its original lifetime; listing/removal never extends expiry. Removing an already absent scope returns false. Selective deletion atomically erases its confirmation/linkage tokens and preserves sibling scopes and their account links. Removing the last scope or deleting all erases the subscriber and every token, so stale links cannot act on a later signup. Account-linked deletion remains scoped as in P-2.1a; inbox management can still manage surviving unlinked follows.

Before routing or sending, the future dispatcher must authorize the current request/subscriber and recheck its generation/deletion state immediately before minting; a stale queued job must never recreate deleted consent or account linkage. Signup/management requests still need bounded abuse controls and generic responses. Deletion must cancel queued messages derived from removed scopes in the same transaction, including rebuilding/cancelling aggregated jobs as needed. Full deletion cancels every queued subscriber job. The approved independent daily send marker survives only until the next UTC midnight. Those tables and dispatch paths do not exist here; these obligations are not claimed complete.

Tests use synthetic records in SQLite and actual local D1 for expiry, purpose/inbox isolation, read-only listing, selective/full deletion, concurrent removals, rollback and deletion/confirmation races. No email or service call is made. Postal address, sender onboarding, verified quota/shared cap, secret setup and operator deployment remain future inputs.
