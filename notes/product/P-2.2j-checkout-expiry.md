# P-2.2j dormant authoritative checkout expiry

[PR #293](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/293), stacked on frozen #289 65b409d3. **Needs Bruce for merge; no activation.** Approved policy and visible copy are unchanged.

The new `expireTopupCheckout` consumes only the trusted internal result of the authoritative payment reader. Call the existing revision capture before that read. Evidence must identify the exact account, attempt, offer, pre-tax amount, currency, units, live/test mode and valid checkout reference. Mode is authenticated against the historical offer by the reader, not inferred from a browser flag. The reader proves expired/unpaid Checkout plus either no PaymentIntent, a canceled zero-received/noncapturable intent with no Charge, or such an intent with a failed/unpaid/uncaptured zero-capture Charge. This helper does not contact Stripe or expire a provider session.

One D1 batch checks every captured state, nullable checkout, revision and immutable-tuple field in its first UPDATE. It also rejects any durable claim with that canonical checkout fingerprint, regardless of granted/deleted/retired state. A successful CAS records the verified checkout ID and terminal expired state; the second statement consumes the fence only on `changes()=1`. No increment can overflow. SQL constraint failures roll back both writes. A replay or stale capture changes nothing. Competing first settlement and expiry cannot both commit, and expiry cannot erase a purchase or release a paid claim.

Unknown creation, unverified/mismatched evidence, elapsed time, network errors and unpaid-but-incomplete sessions remain blocked indefinitely. A conflict requires a fresh capture and authoritative read, never reusing old evidence under a newer revision. Terminal attempts are not reopened and their IDs/fences must not be reset. Successful verified expiry releases the existing one-active-attempt constraint, permitting a new reservation with a new identity. If that creation is ambiguous it stays blocked too.

Stripe documents that an [expired Checkout cannot be completed](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/expire); [D1 batches](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/#batch) provide the local transaction. This implementation uses neither an expiry POST nor a real payment. Parent #289 and earlier financial modules remain byte-identical.
