# P-2.1ab Follow lineage continuity decision

## Forward coverage

Start from one exact accepted data commit and public subtree with an independently verified observation time. Choose an explicit UTC millisecond-aligned coverage start at or after that observation. Existing rows can then prove only that the same observation existed by the start. Keep the original `first_seen` text and do not infer its historical timezone. A later run must carry the established lineage through the writer's actual ID-first, raw-URL, board and search decisions, or record the original aware UTC instant of a newly committed database row. Re-seeding is an explicit new forward boundary. It does not certify the missed interval or advance any subscriber watermark.

An offline comparison of [accepted data b0abd45a](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/commit/b0abd45a1f1e3ae2d79e289cc899dd4ce17c6ea6) and [its direct predecessor e66294c5](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/commit/e66294c55f9e5cce25517191ead1866ca64f9d05) found 68 valid shards per export. All 19,968 current open rows used naive timestamps. Writer-order ID and URL matching found 19,914 unchanged stamps and 54 rows without either predecessor key. Sixteen same-ID rows reopened with their prior raw stamp. Public bytes corroborate continuity but cannot establish how a new naive value originated. Closed observations therefore need lineage too.

## Recommended handoff

Use a restricted checkpoint with one accepted record and at most one pending record per writer. The versioned record holds the aligned start, the exact accepted commit and public subtree, the listing index SHA-256, every referenced slim-shard SHA-256, and one sorted tuple per evidenced row. The tuple holds the public listing ID, exact raw timestamp, evidence kind and aware instant. Include evidenced closed rows. The pending record also identifies the exact expected predecessor, original candidate commit and staged tree. Keep the evidence out of public Git history, Pages, logs and report artifacts. It contains no subscriber data, credentials, role text or URLs. It does contain a new durable provenance association and may briefly cover a candidate before publication, so limit access to the writer and recovery operator.

The published sample has 26,977 unique rows including 7,009 closed. Compact uncompressed JSON using one tuple per sample row and the exact digest manifest is 2,541,026 bytes (2.42 MiB) for one all-seed-shaped record. Two such records would use about 4.85 MiB. These are sample measurements, not production limits. A future caller needs explicit byte and row bounds and must decline follow planning if the checkpoint cannot fit.

The [current ingest workflow](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/blob/2a86dd662e3d99ad8ab565cc712ee6edbfeeb2a5/.github/workflows/ingest.yml#L101-L142) runs the exporter and publisher in separate processes. Its only uploaded artifact in that section is an ingest report. The [writer state files](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/blob/2a86dd662e3d99ad8ab565cc712ee6edbfeeb2a5/scripts/ingest_data_writer.py#L14-L27) are committed on the public data ref. Neither is an established private lineage handoff. No private checkpoint facility or operator settings are assumed.

## Recovery and deletion

The future opt-in caller must preserve [#344](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/344)'s full lineage across that process seam. A future handoff seam must save the bounded pending record after staging and creating the candidate commit, before the first push. It binds the immutable candidate and current accepted checkpoint. After [#348](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/348) returns an accepted receipt, promote by comparing the old checkpoint and exact accepted identities. Require original candidate ancestry and exact owned tree entries, public subtree, index and shard digests. Delete the superseded accepted and pending records on promotion. [#352](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/pull/352) can plan only from the verified receipt and explicit covered lower bound.

After a crash or lost acknowledgement, verify pending evidence against the actual accepted Git state before promoting. Delete a definitely rejected candidate. Propose expiring unresolved pending evidence after 24 hours and the last accepted checkpoint after seven days without a replacement. If evidence is missing, expired, oversized or tied to a stale predecessor, hold follow planning while normal listings continue. Recover by replaying exact verified writer-origin and carry evidence through every missing transition, or obtain explicit approval to re-seed for a later aligned start. Raw Git snapshots alone are insufficient for replay. Do not delete or skip an existing subscriber watermark to resolve the gap.

Owner decision pending on the forward cutoff, restricted handoff, access, bounds and proposed deletion intervals. G3 and refactor writer release remain gates. The public archive path, retention policy, publication and dispatch are separate approvals. This note enables no workflow, storage or live writer change.
