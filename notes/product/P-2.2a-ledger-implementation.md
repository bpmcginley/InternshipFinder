## Implemented boundary

`recordVerifiedTopup` is an internal storage primitive with no production caller. Its name does not mean it verifies Stripe: the future caller must verify signatures and authoritative paid status, kind, owner, line item, quantity, server-approved offer, amount/currency and mode before supplying the record. Browser input and webhook payloads are not authorization. No secret, private price identifier or production offer configuration is added.

One transaction claims both domain-separated SHA-256 payment/checkout fingerprints, inserts the isolated purchase and grant only for a new claim, then returns the same transaction's row. Conflicting identity, owner or offer replay rejects; exact replay never replenishes units. Purchase data: account hash, payment/checkout references, offer version, integer amount/currency, granted/available/held/consumed/revoked counts, and local first-grant timestamp. SQL requires nonnegative integer counts summing to the fixed grant. Live/test separation lives in fingerprints; no redundant mode field is stored. These opaque reference fingerprints are deterministic, not salted account identifiers or a claim of unlinkability to someone who already knows the provider reference.

`topup_claims` stores only the two fingerprints and state. It is independent of the expiring webhook receipts and monthly run cleanup. Seeded terminal or missing-purchase claims reject without recreating account linkage. **Terminalization and retention/deletion APIs are not implemented by this slice.** Tests seed their future required state; they do not prove the complete account-deletion flow.

## Required before wiring

- Durable, separate checkout attempts and provider reconciliation. Refund-before-grant, active disputes, loss, deletion or unknown financial state must prevent an ordinary available grant; this primitive cannot decide those states. Apply #226 through a serialized revision/compare-and-swap reconciliation layer, never event arrival order.
- Monthly then invite then oldest-purchase allocation, durable per-run provenance, exact failed-run restoration against current purchase disposition, and no resurrection after refund/deletion. No existing bonus, usage, runs, billing or orders behavior changes here.
- Atomic account-deletion fences, pending-recovery blocking, deletion of purchase/allocation/account links and terminalization of both reference claims. Retain only fingerprints/final state indefinitely. Cleanup purchase records 400 days after zero balance and financial resolution; retain positive balances until deletion. No cleanup scheduler or public retention guarantee is shipped here.
- Approved published terms/privacy, owner-created private Stripe configuration and eligibility, capability/display integration, complete webhook-path review and operator deployment. No HTTP/cron/transport/provider integration, payment, email or extension release.
