The harness now stages pending bytes through reservation, upload and explicit reconciliation. Only initial fencing keys and an optional already-accepted predecessor remain trusted seeds. A witness-fenced native SQLite transaction records the inactive slot, original canonical header/digest, original start/expiry, payload digest, new nonce and exact existing tombstone nonce/ETag before any payload PUT. It rejects another reservation, pending or retired slot and checks the exact live predecessor. SQLite receives no raw payload or lineage tuples.

The upload body exists only in a single-use in-memory receipt. No retry occurs after an ambiguous result or eviction. Reconciliation can publish only the matching nonce and payload digest, after canonical reassembly verifies the original checkpoint digest. Its fresh SQLite transaction compares the unchanged reservation and both original cutoffs. A live tombstone is uncertain because an earlier conditional PUT may still commit. Missing, malformed and unrelated objects hold without changing control.

Once either required lifetime expires, reconciliation conditionally replaces the exact observed reservation payload or original tombstone with a fresh tombstone, then clears reservation metadata through the existing witness transaction. Replacing the original fence prevents a previously delayed PUT from later succeeding. If that PUT wins before cleanup, cleanup loses its ETag condition and the prepared witness holds access. This package deliberately does not auto-abort that ambiguous transition or implement its operator recovery. The reservation and matching physical bytes remain for separately qualified reconciliation. A stale reservation nonce cannot clean a later generation.

Native Durable Object eviction preserves SQLite and R2 but loses the receipt and injected witness. Ordinary reads, upload and reconciliation stay closed until the test explicitly supplies its independently retained witness snapshot. That injection is a simulated external witness, not a durable provider implementation or permission to reopen after real restore. Reconciliation preserves the original deadline and never restarts the upload.
