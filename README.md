# InternScout

Find internships, co-ops, research programs and fellowships for **any UMass Amherst major and
year**, in any US state. Then let the **Auto-Apply** extension fill out applications for you. It
always stops at the submit button, so you check everything and submit yourself.

Free. Made by a UMass student. Not affiliated with UMass Amherst.

**Open the dashboard: https://bpmcginley.github.io/InternshipFinder/**

---

## Get started

### 1. Open the dashboard
Go to **https://bpmcginley.github.io/InternshipFinder/**. No account needed. Listings refresh
several times a day from public job boards, employer career sites and government programs.

### 2. Set up your profile
The first visit asks three short things:
1. **About you:** your major(s), minor(s), class year and graduation term.
2. **What you want:** internships, co-ops, research, fellowships, part-time roles, and which terms.
3. **Where:** the states you want (or US remote) and your work authorization.

Listings are then ranked for you. Your profile is saved in your browser only. Change it any time
under **My profile**.

### 3. Install the extension (optional)
You only need it for Auto-Apply and the Deep Dive profile. It isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet,
so install it by hand:
**[Install guide](https://bpmcginley.github.io/InternshipFinder/install.html)**. In short:
download the zip from [Releases](https://github.com/bpmcginley/InternshipFinder/releases/latest),
unzip it, go to `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked**, and pin it.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer.

### 4. Sign in (for AI features)
Click **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** in the extension or on the dashboard.
Any account works. Sign-in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own page, so we never see your
password. A school account with a verified .edu email (like your @umass.edu account) gets the
full allowance below; other accounts get half. You need it only for AI features (Auto-Apply,
tailored resumes, Deep Dive). Search never needs it.

### 5. Apply
Do the **Deep Dive** once (resume, facts, a few questions). Then tick listings on the dashboard
and press **Auto-Apply**. Watch the queue in the side panel. Answer anything it asks, check the
filled form, and press Submit yourself.

## Cost and monthly caps

InternScout is **free**. Search has no limits. AI features run on the project's own AI account,
so each signed-in user gets a monthly allowance. Current limits (they may change):

| Feature | School .edu account | Other accounts |
|---|---|---|
| Tailored resumes | 10 | 5 |
| Auto-Apply runs | 20 | 10 |
| Deep Dives | no limit | no limit |

There are also small limits on how fast you can make AI calls. The extension and dashboard show
what you have left. Allowances reset on the 1st of each month.

If the whole project's AI budget runs out in a month, AI features pause for everyone until the
next month. Search keeps working. If costs grow, an optional supporter plan may be added just to
break even. Search will stay free.

Hit your cap? Under **Advanced** in the extension you can use your own Gemini or Anthropic API key.

## Privacy in short

- Search needs no account.
- Your profile, resume and files stay in your browser and the extension.
- AI requests pass through our small server to Google's paid Gemini API. We don't store them,
  and they aren't used to train AI.
- We keep only a scrambled sign-in ID, your monthly usage counts and the states you picked.
- Delete it all any time with **Delete my data**, then clear site data or remove the extension.

## FAQ

**Which majors does it cover?**
Every UMass Amherst undergraduate major, plus common graduate programs. That includes nursing,
education, hospitality, sport management, journalism, political science, art history, the
sciences, engineering, business and computer science. Your major maps to fields, like Nursing to
health and nursing. Undeclared, or not listed? Pick fields directly.

**Which states?**
All 50, plus US remote. Pick the ones you want. The Northeast (MA, CT, RI, NH, VT, ME, NY, NJ)
gets the fullest detail. Other states get fuller listings, with descriptions, once students pick
them. So picking a state helps everyone looking there.

**What kinds of roles?**
Student roles only: internships, co-ops, research (like REUs and summer research), fellowships,
early programs for first-years and sophomores, part-time and apprenticeships. No full-time
new-grad jobs.

**Why not Handshake or LinkedIn?**
They need a login, and their terms don't allow automated collection. So InternScout doesn't pull
from them. Keep checking Handshake too; many employers post only there.

**Does Auto-Apply submit for me?**
No, never. It fills the form and stops. You review and press Submit.

**A listing is closed or wrong.**
Always check the posting on the employer's site. Then report it (see below) so it gets fixed.

**Is this run by UMass?**
No. It's an independent student project. Sign-in only checks your AI allowance.
